## What is the Smart Sports system?

The Smart Sports Facility system is a 5-module setup that gives real-time court availability, environment (temperature, UV, air quality), and on-site displays. It helps you check if a court is free or busy before you visit and lets facility staff monitor alerts and logs.

## How do I get started as a guest?

Open the main site or Guest Dashboard (no login needed). You’ll see court status (empty/light/normal/busy/full), people count, and environment. Use that to decide when to visit. See the [Welcome](/knowledge-base) and [Using the Guest Dashboard](/knowledge-base) articles for details.

## Who can use the Personnel Dashboard?

Only staff and security with a personnel account. After logging in, you get access to alerts, the full activity log, and the Security Dashboard. If you don’t have an account, contact your facility admin.
