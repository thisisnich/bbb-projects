## How do I log in as personnel?

Go to the login page, enter your personnel username and password, and submit. After a successful login you’ll be redirected to the Personnel Dashboard. If you don’t have an account or forgot your password, contact your facility admin.

## Can I use the dashboard without logging in?

Yes. The Guest Dashboard shows court status, crowd levels, and environment without an account. Use “Continue as guest” or open the guest/main URL. You only need to log in for the Personnel Dashboard (alerts, logs, security).
