## The dashboard data is stuck or not updating. What should I do?

Refresh the page once; data is sent in real time and a refresh often fixes a stale connection. If it keeps happening, the server or a module may be offline — personnel can check the activity log. See [Common Issues and Troubleshooting](/knowledge-base) for more.

## I can’t log in. What do I check?

Confirm your username and password. Use “Forgot password” if the site offers it; otherwise ask your facility admin for a reset. If the login page doesn’t load, check the URL and your network, and try a private/incognito window or clearing cookies. See the [Login](/knowledge-base) FAQ and [Common Issues](/knowledge-base) for more.
