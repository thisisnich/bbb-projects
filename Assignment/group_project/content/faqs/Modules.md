## What are the five modules?

**Module 1 – Crowd Intelligence:** Counts people and activity on courts (webcam, mic, motion, proximity). **Module 2 – Environment:** Monitors temperature, humidity, UV, air quality, playability. **Module 3 – Feedback Kiosk:** Collects ratings and maintenance reports. **Module 4 – Security Dashboard:** Lets personnel view and respond to alerts. **Module 5 – Court Displays:** On-site screens showing real-time court status at each court.

## What do the court displays (Module 5) show?

They show the same live status as the web dashboard: court name, status (empty/light/normal/busy/full), people count, and sometimes environment (e.g. “Playable”). They’re for users at the facility who want to see availability without opening the web app.
