# Welcome to the Smart Sports System

The Smart Sports Facility system helps you know **court availability and conditions** before you visit. It uses real-time monitoring, cloud analytics, and on-site displays so you can plan your trip and avoid wasted trips.

## Who it's for

- **Young adults (19–30)** using public sports facilities
- **Guests** who want to check court status and environment before visiting
- **Personnel** and security who monitor alerts, logs, and facility health

## What the system does

- **Module 1 – Crowd Intelligence:** Counts people and activity level on courts (webcam, mic, motion, proximity)
- **Module 2 – Environment:** Monitors temperature, humidity, UV, air quality, and playability
- **Module 3 – Feedback Kiosk:** Collects ratings and maintenance reports from users
- **Module 4 – Security Dashboard:** Lets personnel view alerts and respond to issues
- **Module 5 – Court Displays:** On-site screens showing real-time court status

## How to get started

- **As a guest:** Open the [Guest Dashboard](/knowledge-base) or the main page to see court status, crowd levels, and environment. No login required for basic view.
- **As personnel:** [Log in](/knowledge-base) to access the Personnel Dashboard for alerts, logs, and full analytics.

See [Understanding Court Availability](court-availability) and [Using the Guest Dashboard](using-guest-dashboard) for next steps.
